A record of every export request: who ran it, who it was run for, what parameters, when, and what happened. Contains no location data — only the shape of each request.

Who ran it is the verified account that was logged in — it cannot be typed in or altered by the requester. For is typed by the operator at export time: it records who they say asked for it, and is not verified identity. The two are kept apart deliberately, so a note is never mistaken for proof.
